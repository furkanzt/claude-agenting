# What Anthropic's own sources say about Claude Fable 5.2 (vendor claims, 2026-11-13)

Scope: the "what the vendor says" half of a routing decision. The measured half (this harness's own runs) is written elsewhere. Nothing below is a routing conclusion beyond what the sources themselves state.

**How this note was made.** Access date for every source: 2026-11-13. A Sonnet 5.5 agent re-read each primary source and checked every figure against the page text. Earlier draft notes (partly gathered through a summarising fetch tool) were treated as leads only. This time the platform docs pages (`.md` variants), the Claude Code docs and changelog, and the announcement page were downloaded as raw text and searched. The system card PDF (264 pages, dated November 9, 2026) was downloaded, converted to text, and the figure pages were rendered and read as images. The cost-optimization page is 217k characters; it was searched in full, and the passages quoted below were read in context. Two secondary pages (Artificial Analysis) were read through a summarising fetch tool and are labelled as such. The session that ordered the note then checked a sample a second time, by searching raw downloads of four pages for the exact strings: the price rows and cache-read multipliers (finding 1), the two positioning sentences (2), the SWE-bench Pro figures for Fable 5.2 `high` and Opus 5.5 `xhigh` (3), the orchestrator sentences (7), the advisor sentences (8), the two Fable retirement dates (10, 11) and the Claude Code lines on the alias and on a subagent's effort (12). All matched.

Status marks: **verified** (figure or sentence seen in the primary source today), **verified with a difference** (the earlier notes differed; the difference is stated), **not verified** (not seen today; listed at the end, never used as fact).

## What it means for routing (only what the sources say)

- Anthropic says to start with Opus 5.5 for most work and to move up to Fable 5.2 when evals on Opus 5.5 at higher effort still fall short (findings 2, 13).
- The vendor's own cost pages show cases where Opus 5.5 at a higher effort matches or beats Fable 5.2 at a lower effort for less money (finding 3). They also show a case where Fable 5.2 beats Opus 5.5 `high` at every Fable effort level (finding 6) and a case where Fable 5.2 `high` scores below Opus 5.5 `medium` at higher cost (finding 5). Anthropic's stated rule is that this varies by workload and has to be measured.
- For fan-outs, Anthropic reports Opus 5.5 with Sonnet 5.5 subagents at about a third less cost than Opus 5.5 alone, with about the same score, on work larger than one context window. Fable 5.2 as the orchestrator cost 19% more for about the same score (finding 7).
- The honesty and long-context numbers in the system card do not show Fable 5.2 as better at abstaining than Opus 5.5, and the honesty sections test Mythos 5.2, not Fable 5.2 (findings 15 to 18).
- The Claude Code default effort for Fable 5.2 is `medium`; the API default is `high` (finding 9).

### 6. Defect-search benchmark (F1, mean cost per run). verified

Source: cost-optimization page (footnote 8 and section 4). A corpus of about 22 million tokens, 130 planted defects, 10 runs per setting, on Claude Managed Agents.

- Fable 5.2 alone: `low` 0.866 at $357; `medium` 0.877 at $378; `high` 0.909 at $563.
- Opus 5.5 alone: `low` 0.489 at $38; `medium` 0.827 at $200; `high` 0.850 at $236. Sonnet 5.5 alone: 0.760 at $103. Haiku 5.5 alone: 0.577 at $25.
- Against Opus 5.5 `high`, Fable 5.2 scored higher at each effort: +1.6 points (0.3 to 2.9) at `low` for 1.5 times the cost, +2.7 (1.5 to 3.8) at `medium` for 1.6 times, +5.9 (4.7 to 7.1) at `high` for 2.4 times.
- Quoted for the same benchmark: Opus 5.5 "scored 34 points lower at `low` than at `medium`, for about a fifth of the cost."
- The page says the benchmark plots "none of Claude Fable 5.2's five" of its configurations in its chart of nine; the figures above come from the footnote text.

### 8. The advisor pattern. verified

Sources: cost-optimization page (section "Advisor strategy: escalate hard decisions", compare-models table), https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-2/whats-new-fable-5-2.md, announcement.

- Quoted: "An advisor can help only when the executor consults it." In Anthropic's measurements the one pairing whose executor consulted often "scored 1.7 points higher than the executor alone at the same effort, at the edge of run-to-run noise, for about 2.1 times the cost." Where the executor rarely or never consulted, the pairing scored "within run-to-run noise of the executor alone, or lower."
- That pairing was Opus 5.5 at `high` with a Fable 5.1 advisor on the internal coding benchmark: 90.1% at $2.92 per attempt, against 88.4% at $1.38 for the executor alone. The page's other measured advisor pairings used Opus 5.5 (GPQA Diamond) or Fable 5.1 as the advisor. No measured pairing with a Fable 5.2 advisor was found on the page.
- Announcement: "Fable 5.2 can also play the role of Advisor (where Haiku, Sonnet, or Opus models consult it to help solve harder problems) or Orchestrator (where it delegates simpler tasks to smaller models)."
- API rule (advisor tool, beta): a Fable 5.2 or Mythos 5.2 executor accepts only Fable 5.2 or Mythos 5.2 as advisor; any other advisor, Fable 5.1 included, returns a 400. Every executor model accepts Fable 5.2 as an advisor. A Fable 5.2 advisor returns its advice encrypted.

### 9. Effort levels and defaults. verified

Sources: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-2/whats-new-fable-5-2.md, https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/effort.md, https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-fable-5-2.md, https://code.claude.com/docs/en/model-config.md.

- Fable 5.2 supports all five levels: `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max`. Adaptive thinking is always on; `thinking` set to enabled with a budget, or to disabled, returns a 400.
- API default is `high`. In Claude Code, Fable 5.2, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 "default to `medium`." The announcement agrees: "Medium effort (the default in Claude apps and Claude Code)."
- Prompting guide: start at `high`; `low` suits high-volume work such as fast subagents and "scored close to what Claude Fable 5.1 did at higher effort" on coding evals; `medium` for interactive coding and everyday work; `xhigh` for the hardest long-running work where a gain is measured; most of the capability gain is already present at `high`. Anthropic advises rerunning the effort sweep rather than carrying over a setting tuned on Fable 5.1 or Opus 5.5.
- Behaviour notes (whats-new page): prompt lines that ask for less thinking do not stop thinking at `medium` and above; a turn can end before the work is done, more often at `low` and `medium`. The "Finishing work" system-prompt text halved unfinished sessions in a replay test and raised output tokens by about 30%.

### 10. Release date, specs, retirement of Fable 5.2. verified

Sources: https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/fable-5-2/overview.md ("Released November 9, 2026"), https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/models/overview.md, https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/model-deprecations.md, system card cover (November 9, 2026).

- 1M-token context (default and maximum), 128k max output, knowledge cutoff Jun 2026, comparative latency "Moderate" (Opus 5.5 "Moderate", Sonnet 5.5 "Fast", Haiku 5.5 "Fastest").
- Retirement, "not sooner than": Fable 5.2 November 9, 2027; Opus 5.5 September 22, 2027; Sonnet 5.5 September 28, 2027; Haiku 5.5 October 7, 2027.
- Requires 30-day data retention; not available under zero data retention unless Anthropic expressly authorizes it.
- Refusals fall back only to Opus 4.8 or Opus 5. Failed prefix checks return a 400. `computer_20251124` returns a 400 except on Amazon Bedrock. Forced `tool_choice` (`any` or a named tool) returns a 400.

### 13. Announcement benchmark table. verified

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2 (raw page text, not a summary).

- "A step above Opus 5.5 in almost all the capabilities we evaluated." Output "up to 30% faster than Claude Fable 5.1."

### 15. Abstention and factual hallucination (closed book). verified, with a scope warning

Source: system card section 6.5.5.1, Figures 6.5.5.1.A and B (page 137, read as an image). Scope: the card says "Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 are two configurations of a new large language model", and the honesty and alignment sections test Mythos 5.2. The card does not report these numbers for Fable 5.2 itself. Fable 5.1 is not in these figures (Mythos 5.1 is).

- AA-Omniscience public split, closed book, no tools. Net score (correct minus incorrect): Mythos 5.2 0.72; Opus 5.5 0.58; Mythos 5.1 0.56; Sonnet 5.5 0.35. The text says 0.72 "places it ahead of all previous models" and that Mythos 5.2 "is also the least likely to state an incorrect answer."

- Reading, from these numbers only: Mythos 5.2 is wrong less often than Opus 5.5 and abstains less often (2% against 7%). It does not abstain more. This is a closed-book recall test. It is not a test of saying "the source does not say" over provided text, and the card reports no such test.

### 19. Capability limits and safeguards. verified

Sources: system card section 1.5, announcement ("AI research and development").

- Quoted: Anthropic "deployed safeguards on Fable 5.2 for a narrow set of capabilities related to developing frontier LLMs, such as kernel development on certain ML accelerators. These safeguards should not affect the vast majority of traditional AI or ML development, research, or general coding. Requests blocked by these safeguards fall back to Opus 5."
- Announcement: in accelerator kernel optimization and improving LLM architectures, Fable 5.2 performs "at about the level of Opus 5.5"; agentic coding, hardware development and traditional AI or ML research are not affected.

### 20. Prompting-guide cost risks. verified

Source: prompting guide. A token budget shown to the model changes spend (removing a large budget lowered cost at equal performance in coding tests at `high` and `xhigh`). One sentence about code ownership raised output tokens by about 70 to 80% on small coding tasks. Lines that tell the model to treat an earlier answer as done made it "far less likely to point out mistakes in its earlier answers." The guide has no section on verification or review, and the words "hallucinate", "abstain" and "grounded" do not appear in it.

### 21. Secondary: Artificial Analysis. verified (secondary source, summarising fetch)

## Limits

- These are vendor claims on vendor benchmarks. Several cost-page benchmarks are internal to Anthropic (defect search, agentic coding), single-run in places, and report intervals that Anthropic computed. None of it was reproduced here.
- Best-effort comparisons (announcement table, card Table 8.1.A) are not equal-cost comparisons; the cost-page figures are the only ones that put both models at named efforts with a price.
- The honesty, hallucination and audit figures belong to Mythos 5.2. Whether they carry over to Fable 5.2 with its safeguards is the card's framing ("two configurations of a new large language model"), not something it measures.
- Pricing and docs pages change. Figures are as read on 2026-11-13; the cost-page text is dated by its own footnotes to October and November 2026.
- Figures in the system card that exist only in charts (AA-Omniscience breakdown, audit scores, MASK) were read from rendered pages at normal reading resolution.
