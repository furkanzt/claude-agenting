## 1. Outcome

**The `haiku` alias moved to Haiku 5.5** (`--model haiku` answers as `claude-haiku-5-5` on
CLI 2.1.295). The `scanner` agent (`haiku`/`low`) has therefore been running a model the
table's evidence never measured. Haiku 5.5 honours `effort` (Haiku 4.5 ignored it), costs
$0.10/$0.50 per MTok for prompts up to 100k tokens ($0.50/$2.50 above), and is active with
retirement not before 2027-10-07.

## 2. What was measured

- **Instrument.** Unchanged from 10-06: one `claude -p --restricted` process per run in an empty
  directory, neutral system prompt, Read/Grep/Glob only, thinking on, the answering model verified
  against the cell. Harness version 1 (nothing stored was re-run).
- **Cells.** `haiku-5-5@low`, `@medium`, `@high`, `@xhigh` (exact id `claude-haiku-5-5`) in all seven
  grids. Rows' baselines were first refreshed to the 3.2.2 table (cleanup, dont-guess and verify were
  still on their pre-10-06 cells in `rows.json`). `max` was not run.
- **Tasks.** The 13 existing tasks plus six new ones, all reviewed by an independent opus reviewer who
  recomputed the truth by a different method: `scan-02` (five dated snapshots of one repo, 145 files,
  several first queries over the Glob and Grep caps), `scan-03` (147 files of a Python/React tree, a
  Turkish-character string, a line count, a directory trap), `classify-02` (Fen 6.3), `classify-03`
  (Matematik MAT.7.4), `read-report-02` (a 767-line TypeScript module) and `read-report-03` (two Turkish
  prose documents). The reviewers' notes are carried into section 6.
- **Vetting.** The bar reads three runs per task, and a silent-damage candidate is the best of four
  efforts, so a clean sheet can be luck. After screening and the first confirmation batch, the
  candidates the bar accepted were extended to 12 repeats per task (the rules and when each was
  written: file above, which says some extensions ran before it was saved), with the
  Sonnet baseline at the same N. `decide` still reads runs 1 to 3 only.

### 3.2 What stands out

- **Effort is real on Haiku 5.5.** Mean thinking tokens on scan: 5.7k at `low`, 8.4k at `medium`, 15.5k at
  `high`, 25.7k at `xhigh`. Cost per run rises with it, but only from $0.007 to $0.024 on scan.
- **Per run, Haiku 5.5 costs 4% to 12% of the cells it was compared with:** scan `medium` $0.0094 against
  Haiku 4.5's $0.204 (5%) and Sonnet `high`'s $0.076 (12%), though it costs about 30% more than the
  `haiku/low` pin it replaces ($0.0073); read-report $0.006 against $0.118 (5%); dont-guess `high` $0.020
  against $0.329 (6%).
- **The three-run bar over-accepts.** It named a Haiku 5.5 cell on six of seven rows. Verify `medium` was clean
  over its first nine runs and then failed 6 of 12 on verify-02; synthesize `medium` was 3 of 3 and then 3 of
  6; dont-guess `low` was perfect for nine runs and hallucinated in 3 of the next 27. A candidate that is the
  best of four efforts needs more than three runs per task.
- **The Sonnet baselines are not perfect either.** Sonnet `high` passed scan 32 of 36 (it passed 3 of 3 on 10-06
  and was recorded as the standing candidate); Sonnet `medium` passed classify 35 of 36. The adopt rules were
  applied to the Haiku cells as written, which is stricter for them than for the baselines (section 6).
- **`low` was the weakest or tied-weakest Haiku 5.5 effort on scan, verify, dont-guess, cleanup and classify**
  (30 of 36, 7 of 9, 33 of 36, 1 of 3, 7 of 9). Read-report passed at every effort and synthesis at none.
  Anthropic's guidance says the same (skips searches and checks at `low`).
- **Haiku 4.5 is no longer a candidate for anything.** On the new scan tasks it passed 2 of 6 (scan-02 0 of 3).

## 4. Where the harness's raw output was overridden, and how each decision was reached

1. **Cleanup: `decide` says MOVE_TO `haiku-5-5@high`; kept `sonnet/low`.** The bar tolerates one failed run in
   three on a "noticed immediately" row. Haiku's misses (a split paragraph, an altered word) are not noticed
   immediately, which is the reasoning that reclassified scan on 10-06, and the row's invariant is that nothing
   is invented: Haiku altered at least one word in most runs. This is a judgment override, not a
   pre-registered rule; six extra repeats took `high` to 6 of 9 and `xhigh` to 7 of 9, against 3 of 3 for Sonnet.
2. **Classify and verify: `decide` says MOVE; kept `sonnet`.** The 12-repeat rules failed (section 3.1).
3. **Dont-guess: `decide` says `haiku-5-5@low`; `haiku/high` for `fast` only.** `low` and `medium` failed the
   12-repeat check. `high` and `xhigh` then stayed free of hallucinations, but `balanced` stays on Sonnet
   because: the rules file was saved after the `low` and `medium` extensions and `high` was extended once those
   had failed, so `high` is a cell found by extending further; the whole effort ordering rests on one item
   (Q20); `high` made a confident wrong answer where Sonnet made none. (Whether a subagent applies a pinned effort was open when this was decided; it was tested
   afterwards and the pin is applied, section 10.) Cost and the 36 of 36 are real, so `fast` (about 90 to 95% reliability)
   takes `haiku/high`. A fresh, pre-registered confirmation on new sibling-trap items would be needed to move
   `balanced`.
4. **Read-report: `decide` says `haiku-5-5@low`; adopted `medium`.** The four efforts cost $0.0062 to $0.0072 and
   all passed, so this is a judgment pick: Anthropic's default, and `low` was weakest elsewhere.
5. **Scan: `decide` says MOVE_TO `haiku-5-5@medium`, which is an upward override.** The baseline in `rows.json`
   stayed `haiku-4-5` (what was measured), so the bar compared candidates with a $0.17 cell and could not have
   flagged the cell actually running (`haiku-5-5@low`). Moving up to `medium` comes from the 36-run check
   (36 of 36 against 30 of 36), not from the bar.
6. **Synthesize: `decide` says MOVE_TO `sonnet-5-5@xhigh`.** That override is the 10-06 one and stands; Haiku 5.5
   adds nothing to it.

## 6. Limits

- **Harness shape.** Read, Grep and Glob only, thinking on, one prompt shape, `--effort` passed explicitly. The
  real `scanner` has Bash. A same-day follow-up (section 10) confirmed that a Workflow `agent()` option and a
  plugin agent's frontmatter effort are applied, and that an unpinned Haiku agent runs at its default
  `medium`. `CLAUDE_CODE_EFFORT_LEVEL` and `maxEffortLevel` (neither is set here) would override a pin, and
  subagents inherit the session's thinking setting. Nothing here measures the agent prompts.
- **CLI versions are mixed.** Every Haiku 5.5 run and the extension runs (#4 and up) of the baselines ran on CLI
  2.1.295, but runs #1 to 3 of the Sonnet baselines predate it (2.1.289 or 2.1.290, or unrecorded), and 2.1.295
  changed Glob (hidden and ignored files included, exact total printed when it truncates). The harness version
  was not bumped, so "unchanged instrument" in section 2 means the runner prompt and flags only.
- **Tasks per row.** Three for each row except cleanup (one). Statistics here count runs, but repeats on one task
  are correlated; at task level the evidence is three tasks, and for dont-guess only dont-guess-02 separates the
  efforts, through one item. The per-run bounds (36 of 36 below about 8%, 0 of 408 below about 0.7%) are
  arithmetic on runs and items, not 95 to 99% reliability claims.
- **New tasks.** The reviewers rate classify-02's difficulty low (most items carry their deciding noun) and its
  NONE items weakly decoying; read-report-02's module is the ancestor of read-report-01's, so the two are not
  fully independent evidence; read-report-03 was reworked after a review found fewer than six hard questions
  and that rework was not re-reviewed. The read-report tasks are exact-answer lookups that do not separate any
  cells (even Haiku 4.5 passes): they support "Haiku 5.5 matches Sonnet on exact lookups" and cannot rank
  efforts; open-ended "what does this do" reports were not measured.
- **Dont-guess** rests on two content domains (MEB curriculum material and game-project documents) and typed
  near-miss items (sibling, partial, intent) appear only in dont-guess-02. There is no paired near-miss set.
- **Selection.** Each adopted cell is the best of four efforts. The 12-repeat extension and the symmetric
  baselines reduce that effect; they do not remove it.
- **When the rules were written.** `PREREGISTERED-2026-10-09.md` was saved at 13:15Z. The cleanup and
  read-report-01 extensions (12:26Z to 12:32Z), the verify extensions (12:35Z to 12:50Z) and the dont-guess `low`
  and `medium` extensions (12:50Z to 12:56Z) ran before that. The verify and dont-guess rule ("adopt only if the
  cell stays perfect") was announced in the working conversation before those runs; there was no written rule
  for cleanup and read-report. Everything extended after 13:15Z (dont-guess `high` and `xhigh`, the Sonnet
  baselines, scan, classify) ran after the file existed, but `high` and `xhigh` were added once `low` and
  `medium` had failed. The classify rule was written after seeing nine-run data.
- **Production cost.** The measured prompts stayed far below the 100k-token tier (the largest cache read was
  about 20k tokens). Above 100k per request Haiku 5.5 costs $0.50 per MTok input against Sonnet's $2.00, so the
  gap on the BELIRSIZ row shrinks from about 16x to roughly 4x on input, before Haiku's larger thinking output.
- **`quality` disposition** was not touched: it still pays for Opus on synthesis, don't-guess and verify.
- **Not measured:** Haiku 5.5 with `max` effort, `thinking: disabled`, Bash, long prompts above 100k tokens, and
  the unmeasured agents (architecture, security, incident, orchestration), which keep their pins.
